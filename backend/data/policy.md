# Flight Disruption Management & Passenger Care Policy (Policy Ref: POL-2026-AV1)

## 1. Overview and Applicability
This policy governs airline operational responses, rebooking protocols, care provisioning, and passenger communications during flight delays, cancellations, and missed connections. All automated agents and operations coordinators must adhere strictly to these guidelines.

---

## 2. Connecting Flight Missed Connection Protocol
- **Minimum Connection Time (MCT):** Standard domestic-to-international MCT at hub airports is 90 minutes. Domestic-to-domestic MCT is 45 minutes.
- **Connection Risk Evaluation:**
  - If expected revised arrival time leaves less than MCT before the connecting flight's scheduled departure, the connection is deemed **CRITICAL AT RISK**.
  - Immediate proactive rebooking onto the next available connecting flight must be initiated.
  - If rebooking extends total travel time by > 4 hours, airport lounge access or meal vouchers must be automatically attached to the passenger profile.

---

## 3. Passenger Priority & Escalation Matrix
Rebooking and care allocation during constrained capacity follow a strict priority ordering:

1. **Tier 1 (Immediate Human Escort / Urgent Flag):**
   - Unaccompanied Minors (UMNR - passengers under 12 travelling alone). Ground staff escort must be assigned immediately.
   - Passengers requiring medical clearance or active oxygen (WCHC / MEDA).
   - Passengers with connecting international flights where delay exceeds 6 hours.
2. **Tier 2 (High Priority Automated Rebook):**
   - Passengers with infants/small children (INF / FAM).
   - Passengers requiring wheelchair mobility assistance (WCHR / WCHS).
   - Platinum and Gold frequent flyer loyalty tier members.
   - Business Class passengers.
3. **Tier 3 (Standard Automated Rebook):**
   - Regular economy passengers with or without non-critical connections.

---

## 4. Care Vouchers, Compensation & Overnight Accommodation
Applicable based on total delay duration at final destination:
- **Delay 2 to 4 Hours:** Provision of refreshment & beverage vouchers (INR 500 / $10 value).
- **Delay 4 to 6 Hours:** Full meal voucher (INR 1200 / $25 value) plus complimentary airport lounge access.
- **Delay > 6 Hours or Overnight Delay:**
  - Mandatory hotel accommodation at partner airport hotel (4-star or equivalent).
  - Round-trip airport transfer ground shuttle.
  - Cash or flight credit voucher compensation as per aviation regulatory mandate (INR 5,000 for domestic, up to $300 for international).
  - Full refund option available upon passenger request if passenger chooses not to fly.

---

## 5. Partner Airline Interline Rebooking Rules
- Automated system should first search for available seats on prime carrier inventory (`6E` coded flights).
- If no prime carrier seats exist within 4 hours of original schedule, interline rebooking onto codeshare and partner carriers (e.g. Air India `AI`, Vistara `UK`) is authorized without requiring prior supervisor approval for Business/Platinum passengers or Tier 1 special needs cases.

---

## 6. Passenger Communication Guidelines & Grounding Standards
- All customer notifications (SMS/Email/App alert) must maintain an empathetic, clear, professional tone.
- Notifications **MUST** explicitly state:
  1. Original flight number and cause of delay/cancellation.
  2. Confirmed or recommended new flight details (flight number, departure time, seat tier).
  3. Entitlements granted (meal voucher, lounge pass, hotel accommodation, ground escort details).
  4. Clear next steps (e.g., "Proceed to Customer Service Desk B for hotel voucher" or "Boarding pass updated in app").
